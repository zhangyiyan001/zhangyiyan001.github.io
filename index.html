Yiyan Zhang

Artificial intelligence for Remote Sensing

I am a faculty member at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Nantong University, and a member of the Uncertainty Artificial Intelligence and Brain Cognition Research Team led by Prof. Weiping Ding.

I received my Ph.D. from Hohai University under the supervision of Prof. Hongmin Gao and Prof. Chenming Li. My research focuses on multimodal remote sensing classification, change detection, and efficient deep learning architectures.
